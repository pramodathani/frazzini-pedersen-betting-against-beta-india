# Betting Against Beta in India

This study replicates Frazzini and Pedersen (2014), *Betting Against Beta*, Journal of Financial Economics 111(1), 1-25, on shares listed on the National Stock Exchange of India: the beta-sorted portfolios and the betting-against-beta (BAB) factor of the paper's equity tables, then long-only low-beta portfolios an Indian investor could hold, measured after Indian trading costs against the NIFTY 500 total-return index.

**In one line:** work in progress; no results yet.
